### Interpretation

- **Sheet:** rank mapping contributes to the displayed character sheet or relevant weapon-derived number.
- **Partial:** a scoped bonus or passive is represented, but other effects require a combat, aura, resource, or pet model.
- **Other labels:** identified from the talent's published maximum-rank description and kept deliberately out of permanent global statistics until scoped mechanics are implemented and verified.

### Remaining validation gates

1. Verify the mapped passive percentages, rounding, and stacking in the live OctoWoW client.
2. Cross-check weapon subclass/form/pet and aura eligibility. No conditional effect should be treated as always active.
3. Implement exact spell-family, ranged-attack, and target-stat models; school bonuses are currently shown separately.
4. Add encounter simulation, event-driven proc uptime, and ability-specific damage and healing before presenting DPS/TPS/HPS comparisons.
5. Extend mappings for every outstanding talent where a permanent, testable sheet contribution can be demonstrated.

**Branch isolation:** This branch contains one class's authoritative effect registry. Do not merge several branches without consolidating their `talent-rules.mjs` contents into a multi-class registry; a direct later merge could replace earlier class mappings.
