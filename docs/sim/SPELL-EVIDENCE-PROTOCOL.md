# Phase 1 spell evidence
The review-spell-evidence CLI ingests exact numeric observations keyed by the queued conflict field, spell identity and rank. Keep realm, phase, client build, unit and controlled conditions separate. Provide independently authenticated raw evidence, no inferred spell ID from spellbook index. Different results remain conflicts; matching independent observations are candidates only. A field candidate does not promote the spell or change its runtime inputs. This first reviewer is intentionally restricted to deterministic numeric conflicts: categorical availability, multi-field conflicts, damage distributions and inferred coefficients need dedicated review. Capture exact costs/cooldowns under controlled talents, gear and buffs; use repeated normal/critical outcomes at multiple stat values for coefficient inference. No synthetic fixture is a live measurement.
