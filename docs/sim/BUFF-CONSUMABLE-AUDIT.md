## Scope and evidence
- All 1,302 imported consumable records passed the classifier. 192 explicit long-duration/prepull vectors, four scoped contributions, 38 short-duration events, 726 event/other non-prepull records (classification does not establish combat implementation), 74 enhancement records, 24 recipes, eight utility effects and 229 records without usable verified effect text. Six long-duration records omit numerical values; the last previously unmapped food (42162) omits a unit and is now flagged unquantified.
- All 56 shared choices have status: 23 imported source-prepull, 9 spell-source-backed (some assume improved provider talent), 14 reference-static, 3 reference-scoped, 7 event. No mapped status means verified live uptime, stacking, or combat accuracy.
- Exact item effects now distinguish damage-only and healing-only spell power; include armour, resistances, food MP5 and HP5, both attributes in compound descriptions, and negative modifiers. Short duration effects are not averaged. The selected long-duration buffs are assumed active at pull, not maintained for the full duration. Additional item effects with unknown magnitudes are not assigned values.
- Shared alias corrections: Medivh's Merlot (61174); Rumsey Rum Black Label (21151), not Rumsey Rum (17048); Dirge's Kickin' Chimaerok Chops (21023). Potion of Quickness (61181) is timed +5% haste for 30 seconds, not static +1.25%.
- Weapons without actual compatible equipment or with active conflicting poison/imbue receive no oil stat bonus. Exclusive groups remain reference assumptions except explicit flask/Zanza text; duplicate selections normalised without conflict false positives. Shared cooldown is not evidence of exclusivity.
- Direct spell source evidence (2026-11-17): Battle Shout R7 (spell 25289, 232 melee AP); Blessing of Might R7 (25291, 185) and Improved Blessing of Might (20048, 20%); Kings (20217, 10% total stats); Gift of the Wild R2 (21850, 12 all attributes, 285 armour, 20 resistances); Grace of Air R3 (25359, 77 agility, with 25% Enhancing Totems from imported Shaman talent 262, assumed rounded down to 96); Sunder R5 (11597, 450 per stack ×5); Expose R5 (11198, 1700 at 5 combo points, improved ×1.5 from Rogue talent 147); Curse of Recklessness R4 (11717, -640 target armour, +90 target AP); Feral Faerie Fire R4 (17392, -505 target armour). Each canonical URL is https://octowow.st/db/?spell=<id>. These are tooltip-derived values with explicitly stated provider assumptions. No independent server combat capture.
- Split non-item armour from item armour for Toughness/Thick Hide/form multiplier calculations, and preserve target debuff contributions separately. Do not infer final damage from target modifiers.
